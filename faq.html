FAQ

Answers to the questions we actually get.

What is EndEver Live?

It is the VOD HQ and live TV. We host the recordings of streams and the links out to where they live. Event forms and sign-ups happen outside of this site — creators post those links themselves.

Do I need an account to watch?

No. Everything public is open. You only need an account to post, apply to become a creator, or see members-only videos.

What are members-only videos?

Videos a creator has marked as exclusive to members. Non-members see the page, the title and the description — but not the video. Membership is granted by linking an active YouTube membership, Twitch subscription or Patreon membership.

How do I become a creator?

Apply through the Become a Creator page. Tell us what you do, link your work, and lay out your plan. A human reads every application. Posting is never open to the public — all creators are whitelisted and verified.

Who owns the content I post?

You do. Ownership stays with the creator posting the content. We host the VOD and the link out. We do not claim your work.

Can I pull my content?

Yes. Creators keep the right to pull their content. EndEver also keeps the right to pull content. Either side can do it.

What if YouTube removes my video?

If a public platform pulls content that both you and EndEver still want to promote, we will put it back up without any third-party server and host it ourselves. We will not let any platform hold our creators back.

Do you use AI content?

No. Everything here is made by a person.

Do you track me?

No. We do not track you, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell or share anything about you. Third parties like YouTube and Google have their own tracking for their players — if YouTube players are used here, we will never make you log in to a Google account to use one.

What happens if I am banned elsewhere?

A ban on any EndEver platform or space will automatically hide your posted content from EndEver Live. It is not gone — it just will not show here until the ban is lifted.

Where do event sign-ups happen?

Outside of EndEver Live. This site is the VOD HQ and live TV. Creators are responsible for posting the links to their own event sign-ups.

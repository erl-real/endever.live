Become a Creator

EndEver Live is built by people who already make things. If you produce, teach, host or interview, you can apply to post here.

The application asks for your existing work, your socials, a one, two and five year plan, and the craft behind it — instruments, DAWs, gear, schooling, bands, labels. Everything lands in one place on your profile.

A human reads every application. We do not auto-approve, and your application is never published.

Memberships

Some videos are members only. Non-members see the page, the title and the description — but not the video. The stream itself always lives where it lives; this is the VOD and the link out.

Membership is granted through the platforms you already support us on. Link an active YouTube membership, Twitch subscription or Patreon membership and it carries over here.

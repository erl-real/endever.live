Terms, Privacy & Cookies

Three short documents. Read whichever one applies to you.

Terms

What this site is

EndEver Live is the VOD HQ and live TV. Event forms and sign-ups happen outside of endever.live — creators are responsible for posting the links to their own event sign-ups.

Public platforms

We use public platforms like YouTube so creators can control and retain their own brand, with or without EndEver Live, long term. You are not posting into a walled garden. Your work lives on your channel first.

Ownership

Ownership of content stays with the creator posting the content. We host the VOD and the link out. We do not claim your work.

Member-only and exclusivity

Creators control what content is member-only, and for how long it is exclusive. That decision is yours alone.

Pulling content

Creators keep the right to pull their content. EndEver keeps the right to pull content. Either side can do it, and we will tell you why when we do.

If a platform pulls your content

If YouTube or any public platform pulls content that both the creator and EndEver still want to promote, we will put it back up without any third-party server and host it ourselves. We will not let any platform hold our creators back.

No AI content

No AI-generated content. Everything here is made by a person.

Verified creators only

All creators are whitelisted and verified. Posting content is never open to the public.

Bans

A ban on any EndEver platform or space will automatically hide your posted content from EndEver Live. It is not gone — it just will not show here until the ban is lifted.

Privacy

You control your data

We do not need your data. You control it. If you want it gone, it is gone.

What we store

Your account, the content you post, and the application you submitted to become a creator. That is the extent of it.

What we never do

We never track you. We do not build profiles. We do not sell, share or trade anything about you.

Third parties

Third parties like YouTube and Google have their own tracking for their players. If YouTube players are used here, we will never make you log in to a Google account to use a YouTube player.

If YouTube changes

If YouTube ever changes, we will replace the use of their platform here.

Cookies

Simple things

We use a small number of cookies for loading optimization — keeping you signed in, remembering your preferences, making the site faster.

We never track

We do not need data. We do not track you. You control your data.

Third parties

Third parties like YouTube and Google have some tracking for their own players. If YouTube players are used here, we will never make you log in to a Google account to use a YouTube player.

If YouTube changes

If YouTube ever changes, we will replace the use of their platform here.

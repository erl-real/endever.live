Become a Creator

Tell us who you are and what you make. Everything lands in one cell on your profile.

What we expect

Read this before you fill anything in. It is the difference between an application we can act on and one we have to chase you for.

Your content

Link work that already exists. We want to see you are active before we give you a posting slot — a channel, a SoundCloud, a Bandcamp, a Twitch VOD folder. One strong link beats five weak ones.

Your plan

The year, two-year and five-year sections are the most important part of this form. We are not looking for vague ambition. We want to know what you will post, how often, and what you expect to exist in five years that does not exist today.

Your craft

Instruments, DAWs, gear, schooling, bands, labels — only list what is real. If you teach, say what and at what level. If you own it, say it. If you would promote it, say why you would.

What happens next

Your answers are stored as a single JSON object on your profile. A human reads them. We do not auto-approve, and we do not publish your application anywhere.

Already a creator?

You do not need to apply again. If you already post on EndEver Live, this form will just overwrite your existing application.

Sign in to continue

You need an EndEver Live account to apply. It is the same account you use for neverendever.com — one login, both sites.

Bypass is for development only. Nothing you submit while bypassed is saved to a real account.
